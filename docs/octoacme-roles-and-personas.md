# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **QA/Testing Leads** on test automation strategies and acceptance criteria validation
- Collaborate with **Technical Architects** on design reviews and architectural alignment
- Work with **Product Managers** to understand acceptance criteria and prioritize backlog items
- Report progress and blockers to **Project Managers** in standups and reviews

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Report to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on progress against success metrics and product roadmap
- Work with **Project Managers** to align timelines and prioritization
- Consult with **Technical Architects** on technical feasibility and long-term strategy implications
- Coordinate with **Communications/Marketing** on feature announcements and go-to-market strategy

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **QA/Testing Leads** to track quality readiness and test coverage
- Collaborate with **Technical Architects** to manage technical dependencies and risks
- Work with **Product Managers** on scope, priority, and milestone alignment
- Escalate blockers to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** as needed
- Facilitate communication between all delivery team members

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure software quality and acceptance criteria validation. They design test strategies, coordinate testing activities, and report on quality metrics and coverage.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Report on test coverage, defects, and quality readiness
- Identify testing risks and propose mitigations
- Collaborate with developers on test automation and CI integration
- Validate that features meet Definition of Done before handoff

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, defect-free releases
- Maximize test coverage and early defect detection
- Reduce time spent on post-release issue triage

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Daily standup updates on test status
- Weekly quality metrics and risk reports
- Test readiness reports before release gates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Developers** to design automated tests and define test coverage standards
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria interpretation and validation
- Partner with **Project Managers** to forecast quality readiness and identify test-related risks
- Consult with **Technical Architects** on test infrastructure and automation frameworks
- Report quality status to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** as part of release readiness assessments

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical vision, design guidance, and ensure systems are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review major design decisions and PRs for architectural alignment
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers on design principles and best practices
- Evaluate and recommend tools, frameworks, and dependencies
- Ensure systems are scalable, performant, and maintainable long-term

### Goals
- Ensure systems are scalable, performant, and maintainable
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Accelerate delivery through reusable patterns and infrastructure

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review comments on major changes
- Ad-hoc mentoring and technical guidance
- Architecture sessions during planning phases

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** to review designs and provide technical mentorship
- Work with **Product Managers** to ensure technical feasibility and long-term sustainability of roadmap items
- Collaborate with **Project Managers** to identify technical dependencies and architectural risks
- Advise **QA/Testing Leads** on test infrastructure and performance testing requirements
- Communicate technical strategy to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on major initiatives

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, provide approvals, and ensure projects deliver measurable value. They champion projects and remove organizational barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Provide approval and resourcing decisions
- Escalate and resolve organizational blockers
- Communicate project value to broader organization
- Review milestones and sign off on releases
- Monitor projects against success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain executive visibility and alignment
- Enable rapid decision-making and resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status reviews
- Escalation meetings for major blockers
- Release announcements and impact reports
- Budget and resource allocation reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Receive project updates from **Project Managers** on progress, risks, and resource needs
- Review success metrics and outcomes with **Product Managers**
- Receive quality and readiness reports from **QA/Testing Leads** before releases
- Consult **Technical Architects** on major technical decisions with business implications
- Communicate project wins and learnings via **Communications/Marketing**

---

## Communications/Marketing Lead

### Role Summary
Communications/Marketing Leads ensure consistent messaging, manage stakeholder awareness, and coordinate product announcements. They bridge internal delivery activities with external audiences and internal team communication.

### Responsibilities
- Develop go-to-market and communication strategies for releases
- Coordinate internal and external messaging around project milestones
- Create release communications, announcements, and documentation
- Gather and synthesize project stories for broader stakeholder communication
- Ensure consistent brand and messaging across all channels
- Coordinate product launch activities and training communications

### Goals
- Maximize awareness and adoption of delivered features
- Maintain consistent, clear messaging to all stakeholders
- Support successful releases and feature adoption

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with Product Managers on messaging and timing
- Release communication drafts and review cycles
- Stakeholder and team announcements
- Post-release impact and adoption reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Product Managers** to develop messaging strategy and success metrics
- Partner with **Project Managers** on release timelines and announcement coordination
- Consult **Technical Architects** and **Developers** for technical accuracy in communications
- Receive quality/readiness status from **QA/Testing Leads** for release communications
- Support **Stakeholders/Sponsors** with executive communications and status updates
